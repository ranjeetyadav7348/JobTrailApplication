Finds the jobs you applied for and warns you when a test link lands. Uses the same IMAP account as reply detection, opened read-only — nothing is ever marked as read.

AI triage optional · costs money

The keyword rules handle most mail on their own. When they cannot tell a real application from a "jobs you may like" digest, this asks a model to settle it — and rescues job mail written in wording the rules do not know.

Only the ambiguous messages are sent, and only their sender, subject and body text — never your credentials or attachments. Turn this off and scanning keeps working exactly as it does today, on rules alone.
